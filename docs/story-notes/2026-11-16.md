# Run 2026-11-16: the move from A1 to A2

## A2 research (done before writing)
- CEFR Companion Volume 2018 (Council of Europe) and the CEFR self-assessment grid: at A2 a reader can read very short, simple texts and find specific, predictable information in everyday material.
- A2 course syllabus: coLanguage Dutch A2 grammar list (past tenses, future with zullen/gaan, separable verbs, inversion, advice/wishes, indirect speech) and a Colanguage A2 lesson stating that the past is mostly expressed with the onvoltooid verleden tijd or the voltooid tegenwoordige tijd (perfect).
- Inburgering.org: A2 is the level of the civic integration exam (Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I is B1, not A2). I found no official Taalunie list of A2 grammar topics, so the table is based on the CEFR descriptors plus the course syllabus.
- Change made to the table in docs/CURRICULUM.md: the perfect with hebben AND zijn was already introduced in A1 7-10 (perf, sepperf pills), so A2 1-2 now consolidates it (helper choice, irregular participles, vroeger/vorige week). A2 3-4 now carries the simple past of zijn/hebben, the future with gaan/zullen, superlatives and er + preposition (comparatives were already taught in A1). Also noted the word caps: the test allows only min(180, 70 + 30 x difficulty) words, so A2 1 has at most 100 words and A2 1.5 at most 115.

## Story 25: Tulpen in de Keukenhof (A2, difficulty 1)
- Topic: Nature (Keukenhof). New pill "partirr" (irregular participles). Also perf.
- Insight: the Keukenhof, near Lisse, is open only about eight weeks each spring (2026: 19 March to 10 May); millions of bulbs are planted; April is a good time for tulips.
- Sources: keukenhof.nl, The Hague Online (2026 season dates), Leiden International Centre guide. Flower counts differ (about 7 million in one source), so the text says "millions".
- Quiz: gelopen, meaning of "tulpenbollen", word order with zijn ... gegaan.

## Story 26: Wadlopen (A2, difficulty 1.5)
- Topic: Nature (Wadden Sea). New pill "hebzijn" (hebben or zijn as helper). Also partirr.
- Insight: Wadden Sea is a UNESCO World Heritage Site (Dutch and German parts since 2009; the Danish part was added in 2014); at low tide people walk on the sea floor (wadlopen), with a guide, because the tide returns quickly.
- Sources: National Geographic, exploringthenetherlands.com, Wikipedia (several mirrors). I did not confirm the dates on the UNESCO page itself; the 2009 and 2014 dates agree across the sources found.
- Quiz: meaning of "eb", gezien (not "gezeen"), helper zijn.

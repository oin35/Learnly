## Story 47: De Zaanse Schans (A2, difficulty 10)
- Topic: Provinces (Noord-Holland). Pills: indirect (vraagt of), zou (Ik zou graag ... willen).
- Insight: historic windmills and green wooden houses near Zaandam; many buildings were moved there from the Zaan region between about 1961 and 1974; the region once had more than 1,000 windmills; most houses are lived in.
- Sources: zaanseschans.com (official: a public residential area, homes are occupied), Wikipedia "Zaanse Schans", iamsterdam.com. DutchNews (2025) reports plans for tourist tickets, so I did NOT write that entry is free, and no prices are given.
- Quiz: meaning of "stonden ... ergens anders", word order with "of", wonen after "zou ... willen".

## Story 48: Eerlijk gezegd (A2, difficulty 10)
- Topic: Everyday life (Dutch directness at work). Pills: mening (Volgens mij ...), als (Als ik eerlijk ben, vind ik ...).
- Insight: many Dutch people value direct, honest feedback about the work, not the person; newcomers often find it blunt; it differs per person and region.
- Sources: iamexpat.nl ("The perks of Dutch directness", "Cultural differences: Dutch directness and beyond", "Dutch people are NOT direct"), DUB (Utrecht University) "In defense of Dutch directness". These are opinion/lifestyle sources, so the insight uses "many" and notes the variation.
- Quiz: meaning of "bedoelt het niet slecht", inversion after an als-clause, meaning of "duidelijk".

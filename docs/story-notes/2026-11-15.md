# Run 2026-11-15

## Story 23: Met de trein (A1, difficulty 9.5)
- Topic: Everyday life (NS trains, checking in and out). New pill "sepperf" (separable verbs in the perfect: uitgecheckt). Also sep.
- Insight: check in at a gate/reader before the journey and out after; works with an OV-chipkaart and also with a contactless bank card or phone (OVpay); use the same card/device both times; forgetting to check out costs extra.
- Sources: NL Times, DutchReview, Breng/Transdev OVpay pages, iamexpat.nl. The OV-chipkaart is being phased out (reports point to end of 2027) and some details are in flux, so the insight and story stay general: no prices, no dates, no balance rules. The story says "een pas" (a card), not "OV-chipkaart".
- Quiz: meaning of "uitchecken", uitgecheckt vs wrong forms, word order with "gaat hij dat niet vergeten".
- Fixed during review: I first wrote "Dat is hij niet vergeten", which contradicted the story; changed to "Dat is niet goed."

## Story 24: Doe maar gewoon (A1, difficulty 10)
- Topic: Everyday life (modesty). New pill "idiom" (saying glossed). Also perf.
- Insight: "Doe maar gewoon, dan doe je al gek genoeg" = act normal, that is crazy enough; reflects modesty and not standing out. Described as a value many Dutch people share, not a rule.
- Sources: iamexpat.nl "Seven pillars of Dutch wisdom", DutchReview ("doe normaal"), BNNVARA (origin is one commentator's theory, so no origin is claimed).
- Quiz: meaning of "gewoon", word order with a participle, what the saying tells Tom.

## Story 17: De grote dam (A1, difficulty 6.5)
- Topic: Water (Zeeland, Delta Works). New pill "why" (waarom / want / daarom + verb second).
- Insight: after the 1953 North Sea flood the Netherlands built the Delta Works; the Eastern Scheldt barrier is about 9 km long, opened in 1986, gates normally open and closed only when high water threatens.
- Sources: Rijkswaterstaat "Eastern Scheldt Barrier", Britannica "Delta Works", Wikipedia. The gate/pier count and the length of the gated section vary by source, so only the 9 km figure and 1986 are stated; the death toll of 1953 is deliberately left out.
- Quiz: meaning of "dicht", word order after "Daarom", meaning of "storm".

## Story 18: Gisteren bij de molens (A1, difficulty 7)
- Topic: Water (Kinderdijk). New pill "perf" (hebben/zijn + participle): first perfect-tense story, as planned for A1 7-8.
- Insight: 19 windmills from around 1740 in South Holland, they kept the polder dry; UNESCO World Heritage since 1997.
- Sources: UNESCO/werelderfgoed.nl "Mill Network at Kinderdijk-Elshout", Wikipedia. Build date varies (1738-1740), so the text says "around 1740". The "largest concentration" claim was not confirmed and is not used.
- Quiz: meaning of "gisteren", heeft/is/hebben, word order with a participle.

## Story 30: De kaasmarkt (A2, difficulty 3.5)
- Topic: Food (Alkmaar cheese market). New pill "sup" (superlatives: de duurste, het lekkerst).
- Insight: Friday mornings, about 10 to 1, from spring until the end of September; carriers in white clothes; guild dates back to 1593.
- Sources: kaasmarkt.nl (cheese carriers), Wikipedia "Dutch cheese markets", iamexpat.nl. The season start varies by source (late March to Easter weekend), so the insight says "from spring". The superlatives in the story are opinions of the characters, not facts.
- Quiz: het lekkerst, meaning of "proeven", de duurste kaas (word order).

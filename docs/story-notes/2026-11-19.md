## Story 32: Drie zoenen (A2, difficulty 4.5)
- Topic: Everyday life (greeting). New pill "erprep" (er + preposition: er blij mee, er goed naar kijken).
- Insight: many Dutch people greet close friends and family with three kisses (often right-left-right); new people get a handshake; the habit is fading among younger people.
- Sources: DutchReview (several articles, including one on people wanting to drop the kisses), iamexpat.nl, learndutch.org. These are lifestyle sources, so the wording is "many" and "often". The Limburg "four kisses" claim was not used (weak source).
- Quiz: meaning of "er blij mee", "een hand geven", word order with "er ... naar".

## Story 38: Een weekend in Gent (A2, difficulty 7.5)
- Topic: Language (Flemish and Netherlands Dutch). New pill "dat" (dat-clauses, verb at the end).
- Insight: Dutch is also the standard language of Flanders; the Netherlands and Belgium share the Dutch Language Union (Taalunie, 1980; Suriname associate member since 2004); grammar, core vocabulary and spelling are shared; differences in pronunciation, some everyday words and the informal "you" (gij/ge).
- Sources: taalunie.org ("The Taalunie"), Wikipedia "Dutch Language Union", elon.io (regional variation). The popular example gsm/mobiel was NOT confirmed, so it is not used.
- "Ge zijt" is real Flemish usage (gij/ge + zijt); the dictionary marks ge as Flemish.
- Quiz: word order in the dat-clause, meaning of "Ge zijt welkom", dat vs want/als.

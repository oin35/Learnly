## Story 21: Koningsdag (A1, difficulty 8.5)
- Topic: Calendar (King's Day). New pill "noge" (participles without ge-: verkocht, betaald). Also omdat.
- Insight: Koningsdag is the king's birthday, 27 April (26 April if the 27th is a Sunday); orange for the royal house; the vrijmarkt lets people sell old things in the street. Rules differ per municipality.
- Sources: Wikipedia "King's Day", iamsterdam.com rules, The Hague Online (free market), netherlandsandyou.nl. Sources disagree on permits (no permit vs. permit in some towns), so the insight only says rules differ per municipality and the story does not mention permits.
- Quiz: verkocht (not "geverkocht"), meaning of "spullen", word order after omdat.

## Story 22: De verjaardag (A1, difficulty 9)
- Topic: Calendar (the circle birthday). New pill "gezellig" (idiom glossed). Also perf (heeft ... gekocht).
- Insight: guests sit in a circle (kringverjaardag); you say "gefeliciteerd" to close family too; mood described as gezellig.
- Sources: Leiden International Centre, learndutch.org, Expat Republic, DutchReview. These are informal expat sources, so the insight says "often" and "usually". I did not state the song title or other details.
- Quiz: meaning of "gezellig", gebakken vs gebakt, word order with "Straks gaan ze zingen".

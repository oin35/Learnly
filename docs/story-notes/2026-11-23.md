## Story 40: Patat of friet? (A2, difficulty 8.5)
- Topic: Food (fries). New pill "die" (relative die/dat; verb at the end).
- Insight: patat mostly in the north, friet in the south and Flanders (in Flanders "patat" means potato); patatje oorlog = fries with mayonnaise, peanut sauce and raw onions; the origin of the name is unclear.

- Quiz: dat after het woord (not "wat"), die after de man, meaning of "uitjes".

## Story 46: Een bakkie doen (A2, difficulty 10)
- Topic: Everyday life (coffee at work). Pills: zullen (Zullen we ...?), indirect (vraagt of ...).
- Insight: "een bakkie doen" = have a coffee (informal); coffee often comes with a biscuit ("Koekje erbij?"); by about 1750 the Dutch drank coffee more often than beer.
- Sources: iamexpat.nl "Bakkie doen? What a cup of coffee says about Dutch culture", DutchNews "Biscuity things to have with coffee", University of Amsterdam public-history blog (coffee vs beer by 1750). Per-capita consumption rankings were NOT confirmed, so none are claimed. The popular "only one biscuit" stereotype was not confirmed either, so the story leaves it out.
- Quiz: meaning of "Zullen we een bakkie doen?", meaning of "Koekje erbij?", word order in the indirect question.

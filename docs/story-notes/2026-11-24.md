## Story 41: De hoogste berg (A2, difficulty 9)
- Topic: Provinces (Limburg). New pill "indirect" (indirect questions: of, hoe, waar; verb at the end). Mixes perfect, past (vroeg, wist), present and plans, as planned for A2 9-10.
- Insight: the Vaalserberg (about 322 m) is the highest point of the European Netherlands; its top is the Drielandenpunt where NL, BE and DE meet; capital of Limburg is Maastricht.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Vaalserberg" and "List of highest points in the Netherlands", Britannica "Vaalser Hill", iamexpat.nl. Heights differ (321 to 322.7 m), so the story says "meer dan driehonderd meter" and the insight "about 322". Maastricht as capital is general knowledge (not in the search results), which I am confident about.
- Quiz: word order with "of", hoe vs of/wat, meaning of "komen drie landen samen".

## Story 42: De Domtoren (A2, difficulty 9.5)
- Topic: Provinces (Utrecht). New pill "mening" (Ik vind dat ..., Volgens mij ..., gelijk hebben, het eens zijn).
- Insight: tallest church tower in the Netherlands (about 112 m), built 1321-1382; a storm in 1674 destroyed part of the church next to it, never rebuilt; 465 steps on a guided tour.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Dom Tower of Utrecht", DutchReview (towers), Frommer's (one outlier height of 110 m; not used). The story says "meer dan vierhonderd treden" to keep the number simple.
- Quiz: meaning of "Volgens mij ...", word order after "Ik vind dat", meaning of "Je had gelijk".

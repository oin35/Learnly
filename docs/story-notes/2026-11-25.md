- Insight: a large ball of choux pastry with whipped cream, covered in chocolate, from Den Bosch ('s-Hertogenbosch), capital of North Brabant; the inventor is disputed; 100 years celebrated in 2024.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Bossche bol", NOS (2024, "bestaat 100 jaar"), Omroep Brabant (several articles on its origin), DutchNews 2024. No baker is named because the sources disagree.
- Quiz: meaning of "Ik hoop dat er ... is", dat after "zou willen", meaning of "slagroom".

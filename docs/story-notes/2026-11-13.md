## Story 19: De stroopwafel (A1, difficulty 7.5)
- Topic: Food (stroopwafel, Gouda). New pill "omdat" (verb at the end of the clause).
- Insight: the stroopwafel comes from Gouda; folklore says bakers made it from leftover scraps and syrup; the date is uncertain (late 18th to early 19th century); people put it on a hot cup to soften the syrup.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Stroopwafel", Chowhound, Devour Tours. My first idea (1784, baker Kamphuisen) was NOT supported by sources, so I dropped it; the Kamphuisen story is folklore.
- Quiz: heeft/hebben/heb after "omdat hij honger ___", word order after omdat, meaning of "stroop".

## Story 20: Hagelslag (A1, difficulty 8)
- Topic: Food (hagelslag). New pill "dus" (so, normal word order). Also perf (Vorige week heeft ... gegeten).
- Insight: chocolate sprinkles on buttered bread; roughly 14 million kg a year (a widely cited estimate, so worded as "estimates say"); the chocolate version is usually credited to the 1930s.
- Sources: iamexpat.nl "A brief history of Dutch sprinkles", Wikipedia "Hagelslag". Sources disagree on the inventor (1919 aniseed sprinkles vs. Venz chocolate 1936), so no inventor is named.
- Quiz: meaning of "dus", word order with a participle, meaning of "hagelslag" (not a hailstorm).

## Story 33: Bij de huisarts (A2, difficulty 5)
- Topic: Health (family doctor). New pill "zou" (Ik zou graag ... willen; U zou ... moeten), as planned for A2 5-6.
- Insight: in the Netherlands you usually go to the huisarts first; for a specialist or hospital you generally need a referral (verwijzing), except in emergencies; without it your insurer may not pay.
- Sources: DutchReview "Going to the doctor in the Netherlands" (2026), iamexpat.nl (GPs), Expatica, iamsterdam.com. Exceptions (physiotherapists, midwives, private doctors) differ by source and policy, so they are not mentioned. The advice in the story (drink more water) is the fictional doctor's, not medical advice.
- Quiz: the polite request, meaning of "U zou ... moeten drinken", meaning of "verwijzing".

## Story 34: Stamppot (A2, difficulty 5.5)
- Topic: Food (stamppot). New pill "modpast" (kon, wilde, moest, mocht).
- Insight: potatoes mashed with vegetables (boerenkool, zuurkool, hutspot), mostly in winter, often with rookworst; many people make a kuiltje for the gravy.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Stamppot", DutchNews "Six classic Dutch winter warmers", The Dutch Table. The Siege of Leiden origin story for hutspot is repeated widely but not verified, so it is left out.
- Self-review: I first wrote "Lisa maakt een kuiltje" (present) next to "Tom wilde" (past); changed to the perfect "heeft ... gemaakt" so the tenses match, and the quiz quote was shortened to "een kuiltje voor de jus".
- Quiz: moest (past), meaning of "kuiltje", wilde vs wilt/wilden.
